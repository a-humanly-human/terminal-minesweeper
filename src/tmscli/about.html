about tmscli

A terminal Minesweeper, customisable to make it suitable for you.

What is tmscli?

tmscli is a Python module that brings Minesweeper into your terminal. It's lightweight, fast, and built for those moments when you're bored but still want to stay in the command line.

Why it exists

Bored of sitting there, with all your work done, but unable to do anything?

tmscli brings in minesweeper to the terminal and is customisable to make it suitable for you.

Customisation

tmscli lets you adjust the game to your liking — board size, difficulty, and display format. The v0.6.1 release added minor fixes to the scoreboard, and v0.5.0 - v0.6.0 brought a scoring system and colors to the minesweeper field.
